HVDC Studies with pandapower

Steady-state companion to the HVDC Controller Lab: an LCC link in an AC power flow, short-circuit ratio and filter voltage steps (IEC 60909), the reactive power controller over a power ramp, and a VSC link in pandapower's hybrid AC/DC power flow. Every curve below is a real pandapower result — pick a case and the charts switch to that run. The complete Python source is at the bottom of the page.

LCC link between two AC systems

1000 MW link, both converter buses behind a Thevenin impedance. The DC power order is swept from 0.05 to 1.0 pu; at every point two custom pandapower controllers settle the station: LCCConverter (reactive demand from the overlap equations) and RPCFilterController (6 × 120 Mvar banks, minimum filter, ±0.08 pu band, U limits).

System strength & filter voltage steps

pandapower.shortcircuit.calc_sc (IEC 60909, max case) gives the short-circuit power Ssc at the converter bus. Then the six banks are switched in one at a time with a power flow (no DC power) to measure the real voltage step and compare it with the textbook estimate ΔU ≈ Qb/Ssc.

Highlight

IEC 60909 includes the voltage factor cmax = 1.1, so the computed SCR is ~10 % above the design value. With all banks in and no load the voltage is far above limits — which is why the minimum-filter schedule connects banks only as load rises.

Reactive power controller over a ramp

pandapower.timeseries.run_timeseries: the rectifier power ramps 0.1 → 1.0 → 0.1 pu at 0.01 pu/s (1 s steps). The RPC controller switches 120 Mvar banks after a 2 s confirmation delay, keeps Qexchange − bias inside ±band, and gives U-limits priority.

VSC-HVDC in hybrid AC/DC power flow

pandapower ≥ 3.0 solves AC and DC grids together (create_bus_dc, create_line_dc_from_parameters, create_vsc). Station A holds Vdc = 1.0 pu and its Q order; station B dispatches P and holds its PCC voltage at 1.0 pu. 300 km DC cable, 1000 MW.
